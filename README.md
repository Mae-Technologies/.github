## 🏗️ What is Mae-Technologies?

Mae-Technologies is an engineering company with a single focus: **building the operational backbone that small businesses deserve but have never had.**

We're the team behind [**Statbook**](https://statbook.io) — a live business operations platform purpose-built for trades, contractors, and field-service companies. The businesses that build houses, fix infrastructure, keep cities running. The ones too busy doing real work to wrestle with disconnected software.

We're not a startup chasing hype. We're engineers building critical infrastructure for the businesses that form the backbone of every economy.

## The Problem We Solve

If you run a small trade or contracting business, your day probably looks something like this:

- Job details live in **text messages** and someone's head
- Timesheets are on **paper** — or not tracked at all
- Compliance docs are in a **folder somewhere**, maybe up to date, maybe not
- Quoting is a **gut feeling** because you can't see true job costs
- You're juggling **5+ apps** that don't talk to each other
- Cash flow is a **mystery** until the bank statement arrives

You got into this business because you're exceptional at your craft. But the admin, compliance, HR, finance, and project management? That's a full-time job you never signed up for — and can't afford to hire for.

**This is where Statbook comes in.**

## ⚙️ Built to Last

We chose our stack the way a structural engineer chooses materials — for what they'll endure, not what's trending.

| Layer | Technology | Why |
|-------|-----------|-----|
| **Core Services** | Rust | Memory safety without garbage collection. Predictable performance under load. When a contractor relies on your system at 6am on a job site, "it occasionally pauses" isn't acceptable. |
| **Orchestration** | Kubernetes (MicroK8s) | Resilient, self-healing infrastructure. Services recover automatically. Scales with demand. |
| **Deployment** | ArgoCD (GitOps) | Every deployment is declarative, auditable, and reversible. Infrastructure as code, not infrastructure as hope. |
| **Graph Data** | Neo4j | Business relationships are inherently connected — org structures, role hierarchies, project dependencies. A graph database models reality, not a flattened approximation of it. |
| **Relational Data** | PostgreSQL | Battle-tested transactional integrity for financial records, audit logs, and compliance data — the things that must never be wrong. |
| **Messaging** | RabbitMQ | Reliable async communication between services. Events are processed exactly once, even when things go sideways. |
| **Caching** | Redis | Sub-millisecond response times for the data that needs to be fast. |

We don't list our stack to impress other engineers (though if you work in Rust, we should talk). We list it because **our technology choices are a promise to our customers**: this system is built for reliability, not velocity of shipping features that break.

## 🔧 Who We Are

Mae-Technologies is an **engineering-first company**. We believe:

- **Small businesses matter.** They employ nearly half the workforce in most countries. They deserve enterprise-grade tools at a scale and price that works for them.
- **Software should be infrastructure.** Quiet, reliable, always there. Not a product that demands attention — a foundation that enables everything else.
- **Long-term thinking wins.** We chose Rust over faster-to-ship alternatives. We chose Kubernetes over simpler-to-start setups. We chose correctness over speed-to-market. These are decade decisions, and we made them deliberately.
- **Complexity is our problem, not yours.** The platform handles orchestration, data flow, compliance tracking, and AI automation. The user sees clarity.

We're building for the long haul — because the businesses we serve are too.
